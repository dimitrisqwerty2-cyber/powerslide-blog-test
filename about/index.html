About

The Powerslide Blog is a blog about everything rally.

Interesting topics include latest WRC news, Fantasy WRC and ERC routes, Driver and Stage rankings as well as Rally Acropolis history posts.

The blog author is Dimitris Theodorou, you can find me as Dimi_WRC on Twitter.

Some things about me:

I am a Greek rally fan who loves Motorsport, and especially WRC History and rally maps.

My favourite WRC Rallies are Rally Monte Carlo, Rally Acropolis, Rallye Deutschland and Rally Finland.

I do this blog as a hobby, to keep me sane through the monotonous life of a university student.
